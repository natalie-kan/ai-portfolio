About

A recruiter’s perspective. A builder’s mindset.

My career has evolved from Operations into GTM recruiting and AI workflow design. Each step has shaped how I think about better ways to work.

How I Got Here

  1. Startup

    Building the Foundation

    I started in People and Talent Operations at Medallia, learning how the details behind hiring shape the experience for candidates, Recruiters, and hiring teams.

  2. Midsize

    Moving Into Recruiting

    As I grew into full-cycle recruiting, I brought that process mindset with me: understand the need, build strong partnerships, and keep the process moving.

  3. Big Tech

    Growing at Scale

    At Adobe, I deepened my GTM recruiting experience, partnered with Sales leaders, and learned to balance hiring volume with thoughtful, individual conversations.

  4. Now

    Senior Talent Partner

    Today, I bring that experience to AI workflow design, exploring how technology can address the everyday challenges I encounter in recruiting.

Why It Matters Now

Recruiting gives me the context to recognize where AI could help. Operations gives me the structure to turn an idea into something practical. Talent engineering brings those strengths together.

What Drives Me

Make It Repeatable

I want improvements to hold up as hiring needs change. A useful process should help a team work consistently without depending on one person's knowledge or effort.

Start with People

I care about how hiring feels to the people involved. Clarity, respect, and strong relationships matter as much to me as an efficient process.

Test, Learn, Improve

I'm curious about new ways to solve familiar problems. I’m willing to question an idea, learn from what falls short, and change direction when the evidence points elsewhere.

Systems Thinking

My experience in Operations and recruiting helps me see how one change affects the wider hiring process. I look for improvements that strengthen coordination across Recruiters, Hiring Managers, and the tools they use.

What You Can Expect from Me

Honest Perspective

I'll speak up when something isn't working, ask the questions that need asking, and bring practical options to move the conversation forward.

Responsible by Default

I'm clear about where AI can help, where it needs oversight, and when it isn't the right tool. I raise concerns early and make limitations visible.

Enablement That Sticks

I think beyond the build. Clear guidance, documentation, and training help others use a workflow confidently and keep improving it.
